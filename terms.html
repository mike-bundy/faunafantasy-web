Refunds

You buy from Apple, so refunds are Apple's call. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds ourselves, and we never see your payment details.

If Apple refunds a purchase, the game takes back what it delivered. Refunded Starseeds come off your balance, and a refunded Almanac stops its daily deliveries. Cards you've already opened stay in your Binder. Nothing here limits any refund rights you have under the law where you live.

Fair play in the Arena

The Arena pits players against each other with no server refereeing, so it runs on trust. When you play online:

  • bring a team you earned in your own game, with no modified versions, edited saves, bots or exploits;
  • don't quit or stall matches on purpose to dodge a loss or wear down the other player;
  • be decent to people. Opponents see your Game Center nickname, and Apple's rules apply to it.

If someone breaks these rules, we can bar their Game Center player ID from the Arena and the leaderboards, for a while or for good, and we can remove their scores using Apple's Game Center tools. A bar never touches the single-player game or anything the player bought. If you think we got it wrong, write to us and we'll look again.

Online features

The Arena, leaderboards, achievements, iCloud sync and content downloads all run on Apple's services. They need an internet connection, and sometimes they'll be unavailable. We may also pause an online feature, such as the Arena, for maintenance. Almost everything else plays offline once the game's lands have downloaded.
