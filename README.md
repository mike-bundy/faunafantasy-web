# The Fauna Fantasy website

A static, dependency-free marketing site for the game: six pages plus the Privacy Policy and
Terms of Service, no framework, no build step needed to browse. This repo (`~/www/faunafantasy-web`, github.com/mike-bundy/faunafantasy-web)
is the site's only working copy. Everything committed here is published by GitHub Pages from
the `main` branch root.

## Layout
- `*.html`: the six pages (index, world, heroes, play, arena, media), the legal pages (`privacy.html`,
  `terms.html`), the support page (`support.html`, the App Store's Support URL), and `404.html`.
  Every footer links Privacy, Terms and Support.
- **Clean URLs.** Pages are linked and published without `.html`: `/`, `/world`, `/heroes`,
  `/play`, `/arena`, `/media`, `/support`, `/privacy`, `/terms`. GitHub Pages serves `/heroes`
  from `heroes.html`. Page links are root-relative (`href="/heroes#tiers"`, home is `href="/"`,
  never `index.html`); asset paths stay relative (`assets/…`), which resolve the same from any
  top-level clean URL. Old `.html` addresses still load, and their canonical points at the clean
  one, so there's no redirect script.
- `serve.py`: local preview with the same rules (`/heroes` → `heroes.html`; misses get `404.html`
  with a 404 status). Python's plain `http.server` can't follow the clean links.
- `assets/css/style.css`, `assets/js/*`: hand-written styles and behaviour.
- `assets/img|vid|audio|data|fonts/`: generated by `build.py`; committed because Pages serves them.
- `build.py`: stages and encodes media and data from the game checkout into `assets/`.
  The checkout is `$FF_GAME_REPO` (default `~/code/faunafantasy`); it reads its `Assets/`,
  `work/` (store captures, trailer, brand art), `docs/review/` and `Content/*.json`.
  `img/`, `vid/`, `audio/`, `data/` and `fonts/` are wiped and rebuilt on every run, so never
  hand-edit files under them.
- `deploy.sh`: build, commit and push from this repo. No staging clone.
- `_review/`: local review pages (before/after screenshots). Gitignored; never published.

## Workflow
1. Edit the pages and `assets/css|js` here.
2. `python3 build.py` after media or `Content/` changes in the game (a few minutes; video
   encodes are the slow part).
3. Preview: `python3 serve.py` (port 8123 by default, or `python3 serve.py 8766`), then
   http://127.0.0.1:8123/. Links like `/heroes` need it; `python3 -m http.server` would 404 them.
4. Commit locally with plain git (`[WEB.*]` prefixes).
5. Publish only with Mike's go-ahead: `./deploy.sh "[WEB.site] message"` (builds, commits, pushes `main`).

## Conventions
- Page copy is marketing copy; canon lives in the game repo's `docs/design/STORY.md` and
  `Content/` data. When lore or counts change there, this site follows.
- Store copy (subtitle, promotional text, description, keywords) and this site's titles and
  descriptions come from the game repo's `docs/store/metadata_en-US.json`; the reasoning is in
  `docs/store/ASO.md`. The home page's kicker is the store subtitle. Never put a number on the
  lands: runs generate their boards, so the copy says "infinite wild lands". Auto-Battle, wherever
  it is mentioned, is an optional in-app purchase.
- Apple's marketing rules: until there's a pre-order or a download, the home page says "Coming
  soon to the App Store" in plain text and shows no badge (then Apple's Pre-order or Download
  badge, linked to the product page). Apple's product names keep their case (no uppercase
  transform on iPhone or iPad) and Apple Vision Pro never breaks over two lines. Every footer
  carries the Apple trademark credit line.
- The Smart App Banner (`<meta name="apple-itunes-app" content="app-id=6751401088">`) goes on every
  page on release day (or when a pre-order goes live). Safari shows it only when the app is
  available in the visitor's storefront, so it can't be tried before then.
- Counts checked against `Content/`: 100 heroes, 20 jobs, 285 cards, four rarities.
- No vendor-pack art (LoaD/Quaternius/Mixamo) as standalone art. Card art and backgrounds are
  forge-generated (`gpt_image`); cinematic clips and hero "alive" loops are our own renders;
  gameplay clips come from the App Store capture pipeline (`tools/cinema/store.py`); fonts are OFL
  (Fraunces, Lilita One, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next) and their licences ship in `assets/fonts/`.
- Web media: H.264 MP4 at 1280 px or less, muted loops with a poster frame, each file well
  under 20 MB; the cinematics keep their sound and ship their captions (.vtt). Stills are AVIF,
  each with a JPEG twin of the same name that `site.js` swaps in where AVIF can't be decoded.
  Everything below the fold lazy-loads.
- Every page carries a canonical link and `og:url` with its clean URL (https://faunafantasy.com/heroes,
  not …/heroes.html), and Open Graph tags. The
  og:image is the 1200×630 share card `assets/img/share_1200x630.jpg` (`build.py` makes it from
  the wide key art and the logo), with its alt text repeated as `twitter:image:alt`. The home page
  carries the JSON-LD (`Organization`, `WebSite`, `VideoGame` with the gameplay reel as its
  trailer); add `offers` and the App Store URL to it at release. `404.html` uses root-absolute
  paths for everything (links and assets) because Pages serves it at any depth, and it is
  `noindex`. `robots.txt` names the sitemap; `sitemap.xml` lists the clean URLs of the six pages,
  the support page and the two legal pages.
- Studio credit: SideResult Software, Portland, Oregon (https://sideresult.com).
- The legal pages make factual claims about the app (no ads, no trackers, no third-party code,
  saves in the player's private iCloud). Re-check them against the game before shipping anything that
  changes data handling; the evidence list is `_review/legal.html` (local). The site itself must keep
  loading nothing from third parties and setting no cookies, or `privacy.html` must change.
- The hero roster and world data are data-driven from `Content/heroes.json`,
  `Content/biomes.json` and `Content/jobs/*.json` via `build.py`.

## Deploy (GitHub Pages)
Pages serves the `main` branch root (`.nojekyll` is kept). The live URL is
https://faunafantasy.com/ (`CNAME`; the old github.io address redirects there). HTTPS is
enforced in the repo's Pages settings; leave those and `CNAME` alone.
