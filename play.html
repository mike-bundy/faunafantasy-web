The loop

Open a pack. Pick four.
Step through the Rootgate.

Open a pack at the altar→ Pick a party of four→ Choose a land at the Gate→ Win, lose, come home→ Grow your favourites⟲

An expedition crosses three lands, one act each, and every act ends with a guardian. You can turn for home whenever you like. Win or lose, the levels your cards earned on the way are theirs to keep, and every guardian you calm stays calmed.

Battle

Every square matters

◆

Battles are turn-based, on a board of tiles seen from three-quarter view. A strip along the top shows who moves next. Every square has a height and a surface, and some of them conduct electricity.

Take the high ground, then pick your facing

Tap a tile and the path lights up square by square. You can see how high you'll climb and whether anyone gets a free swing as you pass. Then pick which way to face. A blow from the side or behind hits harder, and the forecast tells you by how much.

Height & facing

Strike from above and you're likelier to hit, and harder. Falls hurt, so mind the edges.

Living surfaces

Water carries lightning and oil catches fire. One well-aimed spell into a flooded quarry can do the work of three.

Twenty jobs, eight elements

From Knight to Time Mage. In Emberforge Caldera the locals are fire-born, so Fire only hits them for half. Pack some water.

The Bond gauge

Fight side by side and the gauge fills. Then call your summon: a beast who fights with you for three rounds, or a Wyrm who strikes once.

No nasty surprises

Before you commit you'll see the hit chance, the damage range, any status it causes and who gets to react.

A HUD that steps aside

Commands appear on your turn and get out of the way on everyone else's. Clean, Standard or Detailed sets how much the board shows.

Peril, never gore

Beat a guardian and you've freed it. The violet drains away, the old creature remembers itself, and its land's Wyrm wakes up. A hero who falls is out for the rest of that run and no longer.

Battles play the same on every screen. On Apple Vision Pro the board stands on your table, and you can lean in for a closer look.

The run

Six stops to the guardian

◆

Pick a land at the Expedition Gate. Its painting fills the screen with the guardian waiting at the end, a few of the creatures you'll meet and the landmarks on the way. Then the party sets off along the Trail, a path winding across the land's own blocks, and walks every step of it.

Each act runs the same six stops: a battle, a locked chest, another battle, a night at camp, an Elite, and the guardian. Every win pays Infusions. Tap your party's cards to spend them, and the levels they earn stay when the run is over.

Camp is the party round a fire. Rest, Train or Reinforce, see what Pemberton has on his cart, and settle a camp story. Tonight it's The Lost Hatchling, and the game wants to know who handles it.

Between the battles

Pick the lock, then play the tavern

◆

Halfway through every act there's a chest with a lock on it. Any hero you own can try it, and a Thief or a Ninja has the steadiest hands. Pick it and you choose from three relics; fumble it and you choose from two. Back home, the Snug Root tavern plays Wild Duel with your own cards.

Real-time

Rootdelve: one hero, all the way down

◆

Down here the turns stop. Pick one hero and take them through the Blight's roots in real time, one floor after another, until they can't go on. Each floor belongs to a different land, so Fernwood's moss gives way to Stormspire's copper and then to Gloomroot's violet dark. Your hero's job, weapon and element decide how they fight.

  • Play with touch, a keyboard, a game controller or the Siri Remote
  • Chasms to jump, and walls that fade when your hero walks behind them
  • Chests and drops in four rarities, equipped as you find them
  • A guardian seals the stairs every third floor
  • Most runs end somewhere around floor 13. See if yours doesn't.

Cards

Rip packs, pull Legends

◆

There are 285 cards: heroes, weapons, relics, Tactic cards, summons, charms and crests. Swipe across the wax and the pack tears open. The cards fan out face down and turn one at a time. An Epic turns inside a ring of runes; a Legendary lifts into a gold sunburst, and Seedkeeper Pim gasps out loud.

There are eleven packs to choose from, eight of them Element Packs in their own painted wrappers. Each one shows what's inside, the odds and its pity counter before you open it.

The Binder and the Fusion Forge

Archivist Quillby files every card you own in the Binder, with a count for each kind. Spare copies go to Brindle at the Fusion Forge as shards, and that's how a favourite climbs to ★5 Prismatic, over months. Win or lose, the collection grows.

Money never buys a specific card, and every card in the game can be earned by playing.

Between runs

Home is a village in a tree

◆

Your four stand on Grovehaven's main street with a name plate under each. Drag a hero to turn them round; tap one to swap them out or change their kit. The menu below goes everywhere else: the Expedition Gate, the Rootdelve, the trials, the errands and the shops.

  • Hero Trials: 110 puzzles, one for each hero plus ten Legend trials, scored out of three stars, and a Daily Trial with its own leaderboard
  • Errands: send the bench out in teams of one to three; they come back with Acorns, shards, card XP and sometimes a tale
  • Pack Altar, Binder and Fusion Forge, a step away

The Snug Root tavern

Eight regulars wait at the tables for a Wild Duel: a 3×3 card game with your own cards, where a higher edge flips the card beside it. Each regular unlocks after the one before is beaten, and some play house rules.

Pemberton's Peddlery

Pemberton has a gift for you every day and a Deal of the Day he'll happily talk up. Eight Element Packs sit on his shelves, each with its odds on a tab. Auto-Battle, for the fights you've already mastered, is an optional in-app purchase.

The Arena

Quick Matches against teams of similar power, rated over six-week seasons. With friends, play live or a turn at a time.

Fauna Fantasy
