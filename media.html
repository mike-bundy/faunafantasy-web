Press notes

What this is, for the record

◆

Fauna Fantasy is an isometric turn-based tactics RPG with roguelite runs and a collectible-card Binder, built SwiftUI + RealityKit for iPhone Duo, iPhone, iPad, macOS, tvOS and visionOS (minimum OS 26). One build, five surfaces, controller support, Game Center multiplayer.

Project facts, if you're counting: 100 hand-painted heroes across 20 job schools and 8 elements; ~240 skills; 9 battle boards across 9 biomes; 5 Trail mini-games plus Wild Duel; 110 Hero Trials; 20 original cinematic scenes; 23 music cues; 149 sound events cut from licensed banks; deterministic lockstep PvP with no dedicated server.

Everything you've seen on this site — art, writing, music direction, the site itself — was made for this project. The game is in development; nothing here is launch-final.
