Press notes

What this is, for the record

◆

Fauna Fantasy is an isometric turn-based tactics RPG with roguelite expeditions, a collectible holo-card Binder and a real-time dungeon delve, built natively in SwiftUI and RealityKit for iPhone (iPhone Duo included), iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro.

By the numbers: 100 heroes across 20 jobs and 8 elements; 285 cards in four rarities, each with its own holo finish; boards generated fresh in every land; five Trail mini-games plus the Wild Duel card game; 110 Hero Trials; the Rootdelve; rated Arena seasons over Game Center with no dedicated server; 20 cinematic scenes; 17 music tracks and four fanfares.

Auto-Battle is an optional in-app purchase. Every pack shows its odds before you buy or open it.

Everything on this site (art, writing, music direction, the site itself) was made for this game. The game is in development; nothing here is launch-final.
