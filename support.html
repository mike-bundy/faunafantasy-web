Saves, journals and iCloud

Each save is a journal. Journals save on your device and sync through your own iCloud account, so the same journal carries on across iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro when each is signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on.

On Apple TV, sign in to iCloud in Settings. Without it, tvOS can clear a game's local data when the device runs low on space, and your journals could go with it.

Purchases and Restore Purchases

Starseeds live in your account's Wallet, which every journal shares. Auto-Battle, the Wayfarer's Kit and the Founder's Bundle are one-time purchases. If one is missing on a new device, open Pemberton's Peddlery (the Shop in Grovehaven), go to Offers and choose Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

Auto-Battle is an optional in-app purchase: your heroes play their own turns, against the same foes for the same rewards. Every card in the game can also be earned by playing.

Pack odds

Every pack shows its exact drop rates, pity counters and duplicate protection before you open or buy it. Money never buys a specific card. Where local rules restrict paid random items, packs can be opened only with Acorns, tokens and earned Starseeds.

Parents and spending limits

Every purchase sits behind a parental gate: press and hold, then answer a times-table question. Settings also has a monthly spending limit, and changing it needs a grown-up. Apple's Screen Time and Ask to Buy work as usual.

Game Center and the Arena

The Arena uses Game Center. The game asks you to sign in when you first open the Arena, never at launch. Quick Match pairs teams of similar power. Challenge a Friend sends a Game Center invite, and you can play live or take turns. On iPhone, iPad and Apple Vision Pro you can also start a match from a FaceTime call with SharePlay.

Both players need the same version of the game. If a match won't start, update from the App Store and try again.

Controllers and the Siri Remote

Game controllers work on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. On Apple TV, every screen also works with the Siri Remote. The real-time Rootdelve is easiest with a controller.

Apple Vision Pro

Battles open as a board in their own volume. Drag it onto a table and it settles there. Close it mid-battle and the main window waits for you; choose Return to bring the battle back. To fight across your room instead, turn on Battles in your room in Settings.

Accessibility and comfort

Settings has Gentle Mode (gentler battles with the same rewards), a readable font (Atkinson Hyperlegible), subtitles, Hold to confirm actions, battle and text speed, and haptics. The game also follows Reduce Motion. You can undo a move until you commit an action.

Replaying the tutorial

With a journal open, choose Settings › Replay tutorial.

Your data

No ads, no trackers and no analytics. The Privacy Policy covers everything the game does with data, including how to delete it. The Terms of Service cover purchases and fair play.
