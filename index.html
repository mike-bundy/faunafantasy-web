What it is

Pick four heroes.
Calm the wild.

◆

Fauna Fantasy is a turn-based tactical RPG with a roguelite streak and a binder full of holo cards. Nobody dies in it. Beaten beasts are calmed: the violet fades from their eyes and they wander off home. A hero who falls is carried back to Grovehaven by lanternbugs and sits out the rest of the run.

Turn-based tactical battles

Mind the puddles

Get above a raptor and you hit harder. Get behind it and you hit harder still. The ground joins in too: lightning runs through standing water and fire catches on oil. Before you commit, the game shows you the odds.

  • Hit chance, damage range and reactions on every forecast
  • Eight elements, and every land has its favourite
  • Fill the Bond gauge and call a summon, up to one of the great Wyrms

Roguelite expeditions

Set out for infinite wild lands

Pick a land at the Expedition Gate and walk its Trail. You'll fight a battle, pick a locked chest of relics, fight again, spend a night at camp, take on an Elite and then meet the land's guardian. The boards are built fresh every time, so yesterday's reef won't be today's.

Every win pays Infusions, and Infusions level your cards for good. Lose the run and you still come home with something.

Card collection

Rip packs, pull Legends

There are 285 cards to find. Heroes, weapons, relics, Tactic cards, summons, charms and crests all come in packs. Commons wear carved oak. Legendaries wear sunforged gold, and their holo shifts when you tilt your device.

  • Four rarities, each with its own frame and holo finish
  • The odds printed on every pack, and a free gift each day
  • Favourites raised to ★5 Prismatic over months

Real-time delves

Brave the Rootdelve

Take one hero down under the roots and play in real time. Jump the gaps, roll out of a raptor's charge, and save that potion for the floor that needs it. Your hero's job and weapon decide how they fight, so a Dragoon's delve feels nothing like a White Mage's.

  • A new layout on every floor
  • Loot in four rarities, equipped as you find it
  • A guardian sealing the stairs every third floor

Grovehaven

Grovehaven is home

Your four drop onto the main street in a puff of dust. Swap any of them for another hero you've collected, or spin one round to look at their kit. The Pack Altar, the Binder, the Fusion Forge and Pemberton's stall are a few steps away, and so are the stairs down to the Rootdelve.

The story

When the Wyrms went quiet

◆

The Everwild once sang with eight great voices: the Eight Wyrms, elemental dragons who did not rule the land but kept time for it. Warmed the forges. Turned the tides. Woke the spring. Brought the dusk, and with it rest.

Now a creeping rot called the Blight turns the gentle beasts of the wild feral. It began with a grieving king who closed the Well of Endings and forbade anything to end. The world's endings pooled beneath the roots like water behind a dam, and festered.

A sanctuary village of humans and fauna-folk sends its bravest through the living Rootgate: a young squire in armour one size too big, a tiny grandmaster who eats apples mid-battle, a gentleman fox thief with lightning in his daggers. They're out to calm the wild and free the Wyrms, and in the end to face King Rotcrown, who wanted nothing he loved ever to end.

"He tried to save the rose by never letting it close, and so it never opened again." Elder Mossback, keeper of the Wild Accord

The Everwild

Every land, its own mood

◆

Fernwood has ferns tall enough to shelter houses. Coral Shallows has pirates. Amber Canyons has a field of stone dinosaurs that may or may not be statues. Each land keeps a sealed Wyrm and a guardian of its own, and its battle boards are built fresh every time you set out.

The town

No chosen ones. Just neighbours.

◆

Everyone you lead lives in Grovehaven. Sir Bristleton is a pocket-sized hedgehog knight with a big oath. Tsuki Moonblade only draws her sword by moonlight, and it is always moonlight somewhere. Capy may be the calmest creature in history. Lotl the axolotl thinks splashing someone is a greeting. There are 100 of them across 20 jobs, and each has a card.
