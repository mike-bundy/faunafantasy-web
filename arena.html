The Arena

Your four.
Their four. One board.

Grovehaven's tourney ground. Bring the heroes you've collected and levelled, meet another player's four on a mirrored board, and fight by the same rules as every other battle.

How matches work ▶  Watch a match

The tourney ground

Your team, on show

Your Arena team stands in the arch as four card paintings, each with its level, stars and power, and your rating hangs on a medallion beside them. When the match starts, both teams are dealt face down in their owners' card backs and turned over in a quick zigzag. You know exactly who you're up against before anyone moves.

  • Quick Match, Challenge a Friend and Practice from one screen
  • Win, and the rating change lands right on your medallion
  • Turn-by-turn matches wait on their own plates until you're back

Modes

Four ways to meet a rival

◆

Quick Match

Opponents are matched by team power, and the search widens after 20 seconds. Each hero's turn has a 30-second clock; if it runs out, the AI finishes that turn for you. If your opponent's clock runs out and they stay quiet for 15 seconds more, the AI covers their moves, so the battle never stalls.

Challenge a Friend

Invite a friend through Game Center by message, from your contacts or nearby, or start a match with SharePlay during a FaceTime call. Then pick Live or Take turns.

Take turns

A friend match that carries on after you both close the app. Every hero's turn is saved, and when you come back, your opponent's moves play out from exactly where you left off. Leave a turn for 24 hours and you forfeit; you can always resign.

Practice

An AI rival with a team like yours, on the same rules, any time, even offline. It's never rated, so it's the place to try out a new four, or to play the Arena on a plane.

Fair play

Nobody peeks, nobody stalls

◆

Both players lock in their teams before either sees the other's, so nobody can pick a counter to your party. Every turn is checked on both devices as it's played, and if they ever disagree, the match is called no contest.

You'll both need the same version of the game. There's no server in the middle, so the Arena takes your team on trust: bring heroes you earned.

Rating & seasons

From Sprout to Accord Champion

◆

Quick Matches are rated. Everyone starts at 1,500, and each six-week season hands out titles on the way up: Sprout, Grove Warden, Thornguard, Wildheart and, at the very top, Accord Champion. The leaderboard is your Game Center friends, so the people you're beating are people you know.

Your first win of the day pays Acorns and shards, and the next four pay a little. Matches with friends are never rated. The Arena is for showing off, not for grinding.
