The hundred

No chosen ones. Just neighbours.

◆

The first hero you meet is a fifteen-year-old squire in armour one size too big. After him: a hedgehog spearman, a three-eyed rabbit samurai, an axolotl who soaks raptors and puddles with the same spell, and a dormouse who tends a sacred altar and gasps at every Legendary. Every hero is a card, every card was painted for this game, and in your Binder the favourites really do grow — from Common to ★5 Prismatic over months.
